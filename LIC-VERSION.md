**Error code: `LIC-VERSION`**

## What this means

The licence is genuine, and it is written in a newer format than this build
knows how to read.

Like `LIC-TIER`, this means your **licence is newer than your app**.

## Why it happens

- The licence format gained a field after your version of the app was released.
- You updated your licence but not the app.

## What to do

**Update the app.** That is the whole fix.

The app refuses rather than guessing on purpose. A newer file might *look*
readable to an old version while meaning something different, and quietly
getting it wrong is worse than saying so.

---

## Still stuck?

Open the app's **Settings**, scroll to **Diagnostics**, and press **Save a
report**. That writes a file with everything needed to work out what happened.
It contains no licence key, no signature, no password, and no personal
identifier — it is safe to send. Attach it when you get in touch.
