**Error code: `LIC-NOKEY`**

## What this means

**This one is our fault, not yours.**

The app is supposed to carry a key that it checks licences against. This build
does not have one, so it cannot verify any licence — yours included, however
valid it is.

## Why it happens

This should never reach you. It means a build was published without its
verification key set. It is a mistake on our side.

## What to do

1. **Update the app.** A corrected build is the fix, and it may already be out.
2. If updating does not change anything, get in touch and quote this code. It
   tells us immediately what went wrong.

**Your licence is not damaged and you have not lost anything.** Keep the file
where it is; it will work as soon as the app can check it.

---

## Still stuck?

Open the app's **Settings**, scroll to **Diagnostics**, and press **Save a
report**. That writes a file with everything needed to work out what happened.
It contains no licence key, no signature, no password, and no personal
identifier — it is safe to send. Attach it when you get in touch.
