**Error code: `LIC-PRODUCT`**

## What this means

The licence is real and correctly signed. It was issued for a **different
application**, not this one.

This is not a broken licence. It is a valid licence in the wrong folder.

## Why it happens

- Two of our products are installed and the files got swapped.
- The wrong file was copied out of a folder holding several.
- You bought the other product.

## What to do

1. Check which product you actually bought.
2. If you have both, make sure each licence is in its own application's folder.
   They are not interchangeable, even though they look alike.
3. If you meant to buy this one and did not, the store page is where to do that.

Your licence still works — for the thing it was issued for.

---

## Still stuck?

Open the app's **Settings**, scroll to **Diagnostics**, and press **Save a
report**. That writes a file with everything needed to work out what happened.
It contains no licence key, no signature, no password, and no personal
identifier — it is safe to send. Attach it when you get in touch.
