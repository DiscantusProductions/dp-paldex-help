**Error code: `LIC-REVOKED`**

## What this means

The licence is real and correctly signed, and it is on a list of licences the
app refuses anyway.

## Why it happens

A licence is normally revoked for one of three reasons:

- The purchase was **refunded or charged back**.
- The key was **published or resold**, and is now in use by many people at once.
- It was a **test or internal key** that was never meant to reach anybody.

## What to do

**If you bought this yourself and did not request a refund, get in touch.**
Mistakes are possible and this one is fixable — a replacement licence costs us
nothing to issue. Include your licence id (the app shows it) and where you
bought it.

**If you were refunded**, this is the expected outcome and there is nothing to
fix. You can buy again at any time; a new licence will be issued.

**If you got the key from a third party**, it was very likely shared or resold.
Buy from the official page and you will get one that works.

## A note on how this works

Revocations travel in app updates, not over the internet — the app never phones
home. That means a revocation takes effect when you update, not instantly, and
it also means an old build keeps working. That is the trade we accepted in
exchange for the app never needing an account or a connection.

---

## Still stuck?

Open the app's **Settings**, scroll to **Diagnostics**, and press **Save a
report**. That writes a file with everything needed to work out what happened.
It contains no licence key, no signature, no password, and no personal
identifier — it is safe to send. Attach it when you get in touch.
